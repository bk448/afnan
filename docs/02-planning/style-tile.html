Style tile

The design system for a US expatriate tax firm's website, rendered for approval before any product UI is built. Institutional editorial: authority from typography and whitespace, not decoration. Every value here is a token defined in one place.

03 — Form numbers

The vocabulary of the domain

People search form numbers, not service names. A form number is a typographic object set in mono, never an incidental string inside a sentence. This is the single most repeated element on the site, across 30 form pages, the mega-menu and body copy.

In isolation

1040 2555 8938 8621 3520-A 5471 14653 FinCEN 114 Schedule B

As a link

8621   1116   8854

In running text

A US person holding a pooled foreign investment fund may need to report it on Form 8621, in addition to reporting the account itself on FinCEN 114 and, separately, on Form 8938. The three reports overlap but are not interchangeable.

Note the tabular figures: 1040, 1116, 8938 all occupy identical width, so numbers align down a column in the mega-menu and in tables.

07 — Motion

Hover, focus and disclosure only

170ms ease-out on interactive state changes. No scroll-triggered reveals, no parallax, no entrance animation. All transitions are disabled under prefers-reduced-motion, which this page honours — hover any button above to see the full extent of motion in this system.

08 — Conformance

What this system refuses

The brief names the hallmarks of generic AI output as grounds for rejection. Each is listed here with what this system does instead.

  • AvoidedPurple, violet and indigo gradients, and Tailwind's default blues — the palette is navy ink, warm paper and a single brass accent.
  • AvoidedGradient-filled text headings — headings are solid ink in a serif.
  • AvoidedGlassmorphism, backdrop blur and glow — surfaces are flat, separated by hairlines.
  • AvoidedLarge pill buttons with gradient fills — buttons are rectangular at a 4px radius.
  • AvoidedDrop shadows on floating cards and rounded-2xl everywhere — no shadow on any card; maximum radius is 6px.
  • AvoidedEmoji as iconography or in headings — none anywhere in this system.
  • AvoidedA centred hero over a blurred gradient blob — text is left-aligned on flat paper.
  • AvoidedThree identical feature cards with coloured circle icons — content is laid out asymmetrically and divided by rules.
  • AvoidedInvented social proof — no client counts, ratings or logos. The content architecture is the proof.
  • AvoidedA dark-mode toggle — this is deliberately a single-theme system.
  • AvoidedAnimated borders, marquees and decorative motion — motion is limited to hover, focus and disclosure.
  • AvoidedVague filler copy — every string on this page is real domain language.
  • AvoidedInter as the display face — display is Source Serif 4.

09 — Handoff

Where these tokens live

Every value on this page is a custom property declared once in :root. In the build they transfer directly into the Tailwind v4 @theme block in src/styles/index.css, which is the single place a brand change is made. No component carries a raw hex value.

All page copy shown here is illustrative draft content. No figure, threshold, deadline or rate appears anywhere on this page, and nothing here states that a procedure applies to any reader. Copy requires sign-off by a qualified tax professional before publication.
